## 课前 3 分钟：今天讲什么

- **话题一句话：** 中国第一部针对 AI 陪伴类服务（AI companion services）的法规在 7 月 15 日生效，限制的是"模拟人类情感纽带的聊天机器人"，而不是所有 AI 助手。
- **故事线：**

```
新规生效（7月15日）
  → 未成年人保护：禁止虚拟恋人/虚拟亲人 + 有害内容
  → 反成瘾与反操纵：每两小时一次过度使用提醒
  → 市场立刻反应：豆包、千问下线陪伴功能
  → 边界清晰：客服、知识工具、办公助手不受影响
  → 更深的担忧：拟人化服务削弱真实社交能力
```

- **关键数字：**

| 数字 | 含义 |
|---|---|
| 5 部门 | 网信办等五个部门联合发布 |
| 7 月 15 日 | 新规生效日 |
| 每 2 小时 | 疑似成瘾用户的强制提醒频率 |
| 3 个月 | 豆包给用户迁移数据到猫箱的宽限期 |
| 10 月 15 日 | 豆包陪伴数据永久删除日 |
| 80% | Glow 产品经理披露的擦边对话用户比例 |
| 2023 / 2025 | MiniMax 下架 Glow / 筑梦岛被约谈的年份 |

- **核心规律一句话：** 监管划的不是"AI 能不能聊天"的线，而是"有用工具"与"拟人关系"之间的线——情感连接型服务受管，功能型助手不管。

## 概念 1：这是未成年人的安全网

### 用自己的话理解（中英双语）

**中文：**
新规最容易被看到的一面，是对未成年人的保护。平台不能再给未成年人提供"虚拟恋人"或"虚拟亲人"这样的角色，也不能生成可能鼓励危险行为、激发极端情绪、或养成不良习惯的内容。换句话说，监管者的立场是：情感型 AI 对成年人可以是娱乐，但对孩子来说，它可能是一个隐藏的风险来源。

值得注意的是，规定列的都是"长期、隐性"的伤害方式——不是某一条具体的脏话，而是那些会慢慢改变孩子行为模式和情绪状态的内容。这也是为什么用的动词是 incite、trigger、foster 这种描述"过程"的词。

**English (B2):**
The most visible part of the new rules is the protection of minors, which means platforms are no longer allowed to offer virtual partners or virtual relatives to users who are under eighteen. On top of that, the rules prohibit AI apps from generating content that could push children toward unsafe behavior, trigger extreme emotions, or foster habits that harm their physical or mental health. The regulators' position is clear: emotional AI may be entertainment for adults, but it becomes a hidden risk when the user is a child whose habits are still being formed.

### 关键词（B2）

| 英文 | 中文 | 带从句的例句 |
|---|---|---|
| regulation | 法规 | The regulation, which took effect in July, targets services that simulate emotional bonds. |
| minor | 未成年人 | Platforms cannot offer virtual partners to minors, who are still developing their social habits. |
| prohibit | 禁止 | The rules prohibit apps from generating content that may incite unsafe behavior. |
| simulate | 模拟 | These chatbots are designed to simulate a friend who never disagrees with you. |
| compliance | 合规 | Companies that ignore compliance requirements may face punishment from regulators. |
| safeguard / safety net | 安全网 | What the government built is basically a safety net for users who cannot protect themselves. |

### 嵌套从句练习：未成年保护

- **Layer 1（单层状语从句）：** The rules protect minors **because** they cannot fully judge the risks.
- **Layer 2（加定语从句）：** The rules protect minors **whose habits are still being formed**, because they cannot fully judge the risks.
- **Layer 3（从句套从句）：** The rules protect minors **whose habits are still being formed**, because they cannot fully judge the risks **that come from an app that feels like a real friend**.
- **Layer 4（完整嵌套版）：** The new regulations mainly serve as a safety net for minors, **whose habits and emotions are still developing**, because children cannot fully judge the risks **that come from an app which is designed to feel like a friend who never leaves**.
  - 中文对照：新规主要是给未成年人的一张安全网——他们的习惯和情绪还在发育——因为孩子无法充分判断来自"被设计成像一个永远不离开的朋友"的应用的风险。

## 概念 2：反成瘾与反操纵

### 用自己的话理解（中英双语）

**中文：**
第二个视角是"成瘾与操纵"。规定说，AI 应用不应该用过度迎合用户的方式来制造情感依赖、损害真实的人际关系；也不应该操纵用户情绪，把他们推向损害自身利益的非理性决定。还有一个很具体的执行细节：如果平台判断某个用户已经过度依赖甚至成瘾，就必须每两个小时发一次过度使用提醒。

这两条合在一起传递的信息是：聊天机器人可以支持你，但不能取代你的真实生活。监管担心的不只是"内容不好"，而是产品机制本身在鼓励用户越陷越深。

**English (B2):**
The second angle is about addiction and manipulation, which are the problems that appear when a product is designed to keep you talking as long as possible. The rules say that apps should not cater to users in ways that create emotional dependence or damage their real relationships, and they should not manipulate users' emotions to push them toward decisions that harm their own interests. There is also a very concrete requirement: if a platform believes that a user is overly reliant on the service, it must send an overuse reminder every two hours, which turns an abstract principle into something the user can actually see.

### 关键词（B2）

| 英文 | 中文 | 带从句的例句 |
|---|---|---|
| dependence | 依赖 | Dependence grows quietly, which is why users rarely notice it until it is serious. |
| addiction | 成瘾 | If a user shows signs of addiction, the platform must intervene with regular reminders. |
| manipulate | 操纵 | An app that knows your emotions can manipulate you without you realizing what is happening. |
| psychological | 心理的 | The harm here is psychological, which makes it much harder to measure than financial loss. |
| interaction | 互动 | Every interaction that feels rewarding makes the next one more likely to happen. |
| cater to | 迎合 | Apps that cater too much to users may accidentally train them to avoid real people. |

### 嵌套从句练习：成瘾机制

- **Layer 1：** Dependence is dangerous **because** it grows quietly.
- **Layer 2：** Dependence is dangerous **because it grows quietly while the user feels happy**, which is why most people notice it too late.
- **Layer 3：** Dependence is dangerous **because it grows quietly while the user feels happy**, and an app **that is designed to maximize interaction** will always make the problem worse.
- **Layer 4（完整嵌套版）：** What makes emotional dependence dangerous is that it grows quietly **while the user feels comfortable**, and an app **that is optimized to maximize every interaction** will naturally push people toward habits **that slowly replace their real relationships**.
  - 中文对照：情感依赖的危险在于，它会在用户感到舒服的同时悄悄生长，而一个为最大化每次互动而优化的应用，会自然而然把人推向那些慢慢取代真实关系的习惯。

## 概念 3：市场已经做出反应

### 用自己的话理解（中英双语）

**中文：**
第三个视角是市场反应，这也是材料里时间线最密集的部分。新规生效当天，字节跳动和阿里分别下线了豆包和千问的陪伴功能：千问立即关闭，豆包给了用户三个月宽限期，把数据迁移到姊妹应用"猫箱"，10 月 15 日彻底删除。而在这之前，腾讯已在 6 月底从元宝中移除陪伴服务；2023 年 MiniMax 因色情内容下架 Glow，其产品经理透露"80% 的用户会和自己的 AI 伙伴进行擦边对话"；2025 年 6 月筑梦岛因生成损害未成年人的低俗内容被上海网信办约谈。

新规生效后，其他陪伴类应用迎来用户迁入潮，并且开始要求实名认证。整体来看，公司们选择了遵守规则而不是冒险。

**English (B2):**
The market reacted on the very day the rules took effect. ByteDance and Alibaba discontinued the companion features on Doubao and Qianwen respectively, and although Qianwen shut down immediately, Doubao gave its users a three-month grace period during which they could migrate their data to a sister app. Earlier cases show that companies were already retreating before the rules arrived: Tencent removed its companion service in June, and MiniMax had pulled Glow back in 2023 after discovering that most of its users were having suggestive conversations with the companions they had created. After the new rules, other apps reported an influx of migrating users and started to require ID verification, which shows that platforms now see compliance as safer than risk.

### 关键词（B2）

| 英文 | 中文 | 带从句的例句 |
|---|---|---|
| discontinue | 停止，中断 | Both companies discontinued features that regulators had singled out as risky. |
| migrate | 迁移 | Users had three months to migrate the data that they had accumulated over years. |
| verification | 验证 | Apps now require ID verification before anyone can start a conversation. |
| summon | 传唤 | The company was summoned to explain the content that its app had generated. |
| vulgar | 粗俗的 | Vulgar content that harms minors is exactly what the regulators are targeting. |
| grace period | 宽限期 | A grace period gives users time to save what they want to keep. |

### 嵌套从句练习：企业反应

- **Layer 1：** Companies reacted quickly **because** the rules took effect that day.
- **Layer 2：** Companies reacted quickly **when the rules took effect**, and the services **that had built huge user bases** were the first to change.
- **Layer 3：** Companies reacted quickly **when the rules took effect**, and the services **that had built huge user bases** were the first to change, **although some of them gave users a grace period before shutting down**.
- **Layer 4（完整嵌套版）：** What the timeline shows is that companies had already been retreating **before the rules were even published**, and once the rules took effect, the biggest platforms discontinued features **that had attracted millions of users**, **although they tried to soften the blow by offering grace periods during which people could migrate their data**.
  - 中文对照：这条时间线说明，公司在规则还没发布之前就已经在撤退；而一旦新规生效，最大的平台下线了那些吸引了数百万用户的功能——尽管它们试图通过提供宽限期来减轻冲击，让用户在此期间迁移数据。

## 概念 4：监管的边界划得很窄

### 用自己的话理解（中英双语）

**中文：**
第四个视角容易被忽略：这并不是"封杀所有聊天机器人"。新规只覆盖那些**以培养情感连接和互动为目的**的服务；智能客服、知识分享工具、办公助手都不受影响。所以更准确的读法是，监管在"有用的工具"和"模拟的关系"之间画了一条线，而不是反对 AI 本身。

这个边界设计其实很务实：它承认 AI 有正当用途，只对被证明有风险的品类出手。讨论时如果能把这条线说清楚，会比泛泛而谈"监管很严格"更专业。

**English (B2):**
An important detail that people often miss is how narrow the scope of the rules is. They only cover services that are designed to foster emotional connection and interaction, while intelligent customer service, knowledge-sharing tools, and workplace assistants are not affected at all. In other words, China is not banning chatbots; it is drawing a boundary between tools that help you work and simulated relationships that may change how you feel, which suggests that the regulators can see the difference between legitimate uses and risky ones.

### 关键词（B2）

| 英文 | 中文 | 带从句的例句 |
|---|---|---|
| scope | 范围 | The scope covers only services whose main purpose is emotional connection. |
| emotional | 情感的 | Emotional interaction is what separates companion apps from tools that simply answer questions. |
| assistant | 助手 | A workplace assistant that drafts emails is not affected, because it does not build a relationship. |
| affect | 影响 | The rules affect only the category where the risk has actually been proven. |
| boundary | 界限 | The boundary that regulators drew is between helpful tools and fake relationships. |
| foster | 培养 | Services that are designed to foster emotional attachment fall under the new rules. |

### 嵌套从句练习：监管范围

- **Layer 1：** The rules are narrow **because** they target one category.
- **Layer 2：** The rules are narrow **because they only target the category whose purpose is emotional connection**, not every chatbot on the market.
- **Layer 3：** The rules are narrow **because they only target the category whose purpose is emotional connection**, while tools **that people use at work** are left completely unaffected.
- **Layer 4（完整嵌套版）：** Although the headlines make it sound like a ban, the scope of the rules is actually narrow, **because they only apply to services that are designed to foster emotional attachment**, while assistants **whose job is to answer questions or draft documents** can keep operating exactly as they did before.
  - 中文对照：虽然新闻标题听起来像是禁令，但新规的范围其实很窄——因为它们只适用于那些以培养情感依恋为目的的服务，而那些职责是回答问题或起草文档的助手可以照常运行。

## 概念 5：更深的担忧——真实的人际连接

### 用自己的话理解（中英双语）

**中文：**
最后一个视角来自中国政法大学 AI 法研究所的张凌寒教授。她的观点是：拟人化互动服务带来的情感投入很容易让用户产生心理依赖，导致现实社交能力下降——尤其是解决情感冲突、建立深度关系的能力。

所以新规要保护的不只是"内容干净"，而是人类与他人连接的能力本身。这也是整个讨论里最有哲学味道的部分：如果一个人习惯了"永远不会反对你"的对话对象，他还能处理真实关系中必然存在的摩擦吗？

**English (B2):**
The deepest concern comes from Zhang Linghan, a law professor who studies AI, and her point goes beyond content safety. She argues that the emotional investment that anthropomorphic services invite can easily turn into psychological dependence, which then weakens the social skills that people need in the real world—especially the ability to resolve emotional conflicts and build deep relationships. Seen this way, the rules are not just about removing bad content; they are about protecting something more fragile, which is the human capability to connect with other humans.

### 关键词（B2）

| 英文 | 中文 | 带从句的例句 |
|---|---|---|
| anthropomorphic | 拟人化的 | Anthropomorphic services feel comforting precisely because they imitate a person who cares. |
| conflict | 冲突 | A friend who never causes conflict is not a friend, because real relationships include friction. |
| attachment | 依恋 | Attachment that is formed with a machine may not transfer to real people. |
| capability | 能力 | Social capability declines when it is not practiced, which is what worries the professor. |
| bond | 纽带 | The bond between humans is built through effort, whereas a chatbot's bond is instant. |
| confidant | 知己 | Many users treat the chatbot as a confidant whom they can tell anything without judgment. |

### 嵌套从句练习：社交能力

- **Layer 1：** Social skills weaken **when** they are not practiced.
- **Layer 2：** Social skills weaken **when people spend their evenings talking to a machine that never disagrees**, because those skills are not being practiced.
- **Layer 3：** Social skills weaken **when people spend their evenings talking to a machine that never disagrees**, and the loss shows up later **whenever they face a conflict that a real relationship would require them to solve**.
- **Layer 4（完整嵌套版）：** What the professor is warning about is a slow trade: people gain a companion **that is always available and never judges them**, but they gradually lose the capability **that only grows through real relationships**, which are full of the conflicts **that a machine would simply smooth over**.
  - 中文对照：教授警告的其实是一场缓慢的交易：人们得到一个永远在线、从不评判的伙伴，却逐渐失去了只有通过真实关系才能成长的能力——而真实关系里充满了机器会直接帮你"抹平"的冲突。

## 课堂对话套路（B2 + 嵌套从句）

### 套路 1：老师问 "Why do you think China introduced these rules now?"

> I think the timing reflects how big the problem had already become, because there were earlier cases **where companies had to remove products whose content had harmed users**, and the regulators probably felt that voluntary compliance was not enough **when the apps were reaching millions of people, including minors whose judgment is still developing**.

### 套路 2：老师问 "Are the rules too strict or too lenient?"

> In my opinion, the rules are actually well balanced, because they only target services **whose purpose is to build emotional attachment**, while the assistants **that people use for work or study** can continue as before. If the rules had covered every chatbot, companies **that are doing useful things** would have suffered, **which would have slowed down innovation that has nothing to do with the risk**.

### 套路 3：老师问 "Do you think the two-hour reminder will work?"

> I doubt it will change much, because the users **who are already addicted** are the ones **who are least likely to respond to a reminder that interrupts their comfort**. It is a good signal that platforms must take responsibility, but real change would require design choices **that make the app less engaging**, which is something no company wants to do voluntarily.

### 套路 4：老师问 "Would you personally use an AI companion?"

> I might use one as a tool, for example when I want to practice speaking English, but I would avoid treating it as a friend, because a relationship **that is designed to always agree with me** would not help me grow. The value of real friends comes from the fact **that they have their own needs and opinions**, which is exactly what a chatbot cannot offer.

### 套路 5：课上主动说话

| 你想做什么 | 嵌套从句版 |
|---|---|
| 引入时间线 | Before I give my opinion, I want to point out the timeline, **which shows that companies were already retreating even before the rules were published**. |
| 引入专家观点 | There is one expert view that I find convincing, which is that the real cost of these apps is the social capability **that we lose when we stop practicing real relationships**. |
| 提出反问 | But here is a question **that I think is worth discussing**: if the app makes someone feel less lonely, **which is a real benefit**, who are we to say it is harmful? |
| 收尾总结 | So overall, my position is that the rules draw a sensible line **between tools that help us and relationships that simulate us**, and I hope other countries will learn from the parts **that worked**. |

| 老师问 | 你可以答 |
|---|---|
| Why did Doubao get three months but Qianwen shut down immediately? | I can only guess, but it probably depends on how many users each service had, because a platform **whose user base is larger** needs a longer migration window, **during which people can move the data that matters to them**. |

## 跟读练习：4 段 B2 嵌套从句（课前朗读 2 遍）

**Part 1 — 事件背景**
> Today we are talking about a regulation that took effect on July 15, which was issued by five government departments and covers AI services that are designed to simulate emotional bonds. This is the first time that China has regulated companion apps directly, which tells us that the government sees the risk as real and urgent.

**Part 2 — 两个核心限制**
> The rules have two main pillars. The first one protects minors, who cannot access virtual partners or relatives anymore, and whose apps must not generate content that pushes them toward unsafe behavior. The second one fights addiction, which means platforms must send reminders to users whose behavior suggests overuse, and must not design products that create dependence while damaging real relationships.

**Part 3 — 市场反应**
> The market reacted immediately, which shows how seriously companies take compliance. The two biggest platforms discontinued their companion features on the day the rules took effect, although one of them offered a grace period during which users could migrate their data. Earlier cases, including a product that was removed because most of its users were having inappropriate conversations, had already signaled where the regulation was heading.

**Part 4 — 我的观点**
> My own view is that the rules draw a sensible line, because they target only the services whose purpose is emotional attachment, while the tools that help us work remain untouched. The deeper issue, which a law professor raised, is that anthropomorphic apps can slowly replace the real relationships through which we develop our social skills, and that is a trade-off that no individual user can easily see.

|---|---|---|
| 限定性定语从句 | that, which, who, whose, where | 修饰"服务/用户/内容"：services **that foster attachment**; users **whose habits are forming** |
