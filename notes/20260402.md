## Fixes and Upgrades

focus one -> focus on
中文：`focus` 后面常接 `on`

get to your points / get to points -> get to the point
中文：更自然的固定表达是 `get to the point`

have point -> have a point
中文：表示“说得有道理”时，通常说 `have a point`

out of -> out of my comfort zone / out of the office / out of time
中文：`out of` 常常需要后面接完整对象

I will use these words to gernerate 10 acticles -> I will use these words to generate 10 articles.
中文：修正拼写，句子本身可以直接保留使用

regarding -> about
中文：`regarding` 没错，但口语里 `about` 更常见、更轻松
