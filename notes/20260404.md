## Pronunciation Focus

### 1. blocker

Stress hint:
BLO-cker

中文提醒：前面重一点，后面轻一点。不要把两个音都读得一样重。

Repeat drill:

This blocker is slowing us down.

### 2. retrospective

Stress hint:
retro-SPEC-tive

中文提醒：这个词比较长，重点放在 `SPEC`。可以慢一点，不要抢快。

Repeat drill:

We had a retrospective after the sprint.

### 3. stakeholders

Stress hint:
STAKE-hold-ers

中文提醒：开头 `stake` 要清楚，后面不要吞音太多。结尾复数 `-s` 也要带出来。

Repeat drill:

We need to update the stakeholders.

### 4. estimate

Stress hint:
ES-ti-mate

中文提醒：重音在前面。不要把它读成每个音节都一样重。

Repeat drill:

It is hard to estimate the time.

### 5. immediately

Stress hint:
im-ME-di-ate-ly

中文提醒：重点在 `ME`。这个词很长，先分开读，再连起来。

Repeat drill:

Please tell me immediately.

### 6. critical

Stress hint:
CRI-ti-cal

中文提醒：重音在前面。尾音不要拖太长。

Repeat drill:

This is a critical problem.

### 7. level of difficulty

Stress hint:
LE-vel of DIFF-i-cul-ty

中文提醒：这个短语里主要听 `LE` 和 `DIFF`。`of` 读轻一点。

Repeat drill:

It depends on the level of difficulty.

## Phrase Bank

### 1. at once

中文提示：立刻，马上

Pronunciation hint:
连起来读更自然：at-once

Useful line:

We cannot solve everything at once.

### 2. what if

中文提示：如果……会怎样

Pronunciation hint:
`what if` 一起读，不要中间停太久

Useful line:

What if the estimate is wrong?

### 3. tend to

中文提示：倾向于，往往会

Pronunciation hint:
`tend to` 口语里常读得比较紧

Useful line:

I tend to think too much before I speak.

### 4. refer to

中文提示：提到，指的是，参考

Pronunciation hint:
re-FER to

Useful line:

This word refers to a process problem.

### 5. level of difficulty

中文提示：难度等级，难度水平

Pronunciation hint:
`of` 轻读，重点听 `level` 和 `difficulty`

Useful line:

### 6. project manager

中文提示：项目经理

Pronunciation hint:
PROJ-ect MAN-a-ger

中文提示：产品经理

## Fixes and Upgrades

pronouncation -> pronunciation
中文：拼写修正，正确形式是 `pronunciation`

The student felt struggling in understanding long sentence. -> I struggled to understand long sentences.
中文：更自然，也更适合你下次直接复用

Try my best to use short sentences in the class -> I will try my best to use short sentences in class.
中文：补上主语后更完整、更自然

They just care about if the requirements are implemented, they don't care about the level of difficulty. -> They only care whether the requirements are implemented. They do not care about the level of difficulty.
中文：这里用 `whether` 更自然，而且拆成两句更容易说清楚

If I can finish the story on time depends on the level of difficulty -> Whether I can finish the story on time depends on the level of difficulty.
中文：这里更适合用 `whether` 引导名词性从句

caused by you -> caused by me / caused by the team / caused by the process
中文：这个短语本身没错，但要根据具体主语换成自然表达
