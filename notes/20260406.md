## Fixes and Upgrades

Today first reviwed the words the student made mistakes about in the pronunciation -> Today we first reviewed the words that I pronounced incorrectly.
中文：`reviewed` 拼写修正，句子也改得更自然、更适合你自己复用

I found he has a big improvement in the pronunciation -> I found that my pronunciation has improved a lot.
中文：如果是你自己复述课堂情况，用第一人称更合适

I aksed him some questions -> I asked him some questions / The teacher asked me some questions.
中文：修正 `asked` 拼写，并根据说话人调整主语

He still has problems in using tense -> I still have problems using tense.
中文：去掉 `in` 更自然，复述自己问题时用第一人称

the changes of verbs for the third person form -> third-person verb forms / verb changes for the third person
中文：更自然的说法是 `third-person verb forms`

everyone status/progress -> everyone's status / everyone's progress
中文：这里需要所有格 `everyone's`

out of work / off work
中文：`off work` 是“下班、不在工作中”；`out of work` 是“失业”，意思差很多，要分清
