以下是润色后的版本，使用了丰富的逻辑连接词和过渡短语，让技术选型讨论**条理清晰、对比鲜明**：

---

This article describes a discussion between a product manager and a data engineer about the technology choices for streaming engines and data lake formats.

**To begin with**, the data engineer introduces two streaming engines, Spark and Flink, **as well as** two data lake formats, Hudi and Iceberg. **Each option has** its own pros and cons.

**Starting with** Spark, **the main advantage is that** the team can save significant development time by reusing the existing Spark cluster. **On the other hand**, its major drawback is that it **does not support** true real-time processing.

**Turning to** Flink, **the key benefit is** its native streaming capability, **which enables** true real-time processing. **However**, the downside is that it requires introducing a new ETL framework.

**Taking these factors into account**, Spark is the more appropriate choice, **since** the team can accept a ten-second delay for their dashboard.

**Moving on to** the data lake formats, **both Hudi and Iceberg have their strengths**. **On the one hand**, Hudi excels at incremental updates **thanks to** its ACID support. **On the other hand**, Iceberg is particularly strong at handling large-scale batch processing.

**That said**, each format also has its weaknesses. **Specifically**, Hudi involves a heavy metadata management system, **whereas** Iceberg struggles with high-frequency real-time writes.

**After weighing the pros and cons**, the team decides to adopt Spark and Iceberg to build their real-time ETL pipeline.

**使用的逻辑连接词/过渡短语汇总：**

| 功能 | 短语 | 作用 |
|------|------|------|
| 引入话题 | **To begin with** | 首先，开启技术介绍 |
| 并列引入 | **as well as** | 以及，把引擎和格式一起带出 |
| 总体评价 | **Each option has** | 每个选项都有…，自然引出优缺点 |
| 分项展开 | **Starting with** | 从…开始，引入第一个引擎 |
| 优势说明 | **the main advantage is that** | 主要优势是…，结构清晰 |
| 转折对比 | **On the other hand** | 另一方面，引出缺点 |
| 转入下一项 | **Turning to** | 转向，引入第二个引擎 |
| 核心优势 | **the key benefit is** | 核心好处是… |
| 能力说明 | **which enables** | 这使得…，非限制性定语从句 |
| 让步转折 | **However** | 然而，引出缺点 |
| 综合考量 | **Taking these factors into account** | 综合考虑这些因素 |
| 原因说明 | **since** | 因为，引导接受延迟的原因 |
| 推进到下一主题 | **Moving on to** | 转入数据湖格式讨论 |
| 总体评价 | **both... have their strengths** | 两者各有优势 |
| 对比展开 | **On the one hand... On the other hand** | 一方面…另一方面…，经典对比结构 |
| 原因说明 | **thanks to** | 归功于/由于，正面原因 |
| 让步引入 | **That said** | 话虽如此，引出新一层转折 |
| 具体展开 | **Specifically** | 具体来说，展开弱点 |
| 对比转折 | **whereas** | 而/然而，对比两者弱点 |
| 总结决策 | **After weighing the pros and cons** | 权衡利弊之后，引出最终决定 |

---

**逻辑结构可视化：**

```
引入场景
    ↓
介绍四个候选技术
    ↓ To begin with / as well as / Each option has
引擎对比：Spark vs Flink
    ↓ Starting with / the main advantage is that
Spark优势：复用集群省时间
    ↓ On the other hand
Spark劣势：非真正实时
    ↓ Turning to / the key benefit is / which enables
Flink优势：原生流式实时处理
    ↓ However
Flink劣势：需引入新框架
    ↓ Taking these factors into account / since
结论：选Spark（接受10秒延迟）
    ↓ Moving on to / both... have their strengths
格式对比：Hudi vs Iceberg
    ↓ On the one hand / thanks to
Hudi优势：增量更新（ACID）
    ↓ On the other hand
Iceberg优势：大规模批处理
    ↓ That said / Specifically / whereas
两者劣势：Hudi元数据重 / Iceberg高频写入弱
    ↓ After weighing the pros and cons
最终决策：Spark + Iceberg
```

---

**几个值得注意的地道表达：**

- **Each option has its own pros and cons** → "每个选项各有优缺点"，比 "Here are both pros and cons" 更自然
- **Starting with / Turning to / Moving on to** → 三个不同的"转入"短语，避免重复，体现层次感
- **the main advantage is that / the key benefit is** → 同义替换，避免单调
- **which enables** → 非限制性定语从句，自然衔接能力与结果
- **Taking these factors into account** → "综合考虑这些因素"，比 Overall 更体现**权衡过程**
- **On the one hand... On the other hand** → 经典对比结构，用于**同一层级**的两个优势对比
- **That said** → "话虽如此"，承上启下，从优势转入劣势
- **whereas** → "而/然而"，用于**对比两个事物的不同弱点**，比 and 更有逻辑性
- **After weighing the pros and cons** → "权衡利弊之后"，比 Overall 或 Weighing 更完整自然
