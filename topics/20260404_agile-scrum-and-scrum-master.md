# Agile, Scrum, Sprint, and Scrum Master

## Topic Target

这节课结束后，我至少可以用简单英语完成这三件事：

- 用简单英语解释 agile、scrum 和 sprint 是什么
- 说出 scrum master 这个岗位大概做什么
- 结合自己的工作或理解，说 3 到 5 句个人看法

## Quick Chinese Brief

这个 topic 主要是帮助你用简单英语谈 agile 开发里的几个高频概念：`agile`、`scrum`、`sprint`，以及有些公司会设立的 `scrum master` 这个职位。

你不需要讲很专业的流程，也不需要用很多术语。你的目标是先能说清楚：agile 是一种更灵活的工作方式；scrum 是 agile 下面常见的一种团队协作方法；sprint 是一个短周期的工作阶段；scrum master 是帮助团队顺利协作的人。

这节课的重点不是“讲得专业”，而是“先开口，先说清楚基本意思”。

## Key Words and Chunks

### 1. agile

English meaning:
a flexible way of planning and working

中文提示：一种灵活、迭代式的工作方式

Useful line:

Agile helps teams respond to change more quickly.

### 2. scrum

English meaning:
a framework for teamwork in agile development

中文提示：敏捷开发中常见的一套协作框架

Useful line:

Scrum gives the team a clear working structure.

### 3. sprint

English meaning:
a short period of focused work

中文提示：一个短周期的工作阶段

Useful line:

In a sprint, the team works on a small set of tasks.

### 4. scrum master

English meaning:
a person who helps the team follow the scrum process

中文提示：帮助团队顺利运行 scrum 流程的人

Useful line:

The scrum master helps the team solve process problems.

### 5. teamwork

English meaning:
people working together well

中文提示：团队合作

Useful line:

Good teamwork is important in scrum.

### 6. feedback

English meaning:
comments or ideas used to improve something

中文提示：反馈

Useful line:

Agile teams use feedback to improve their work.

### 7. step by step

English meaning:
little by little, in small stages

中文提示：一步一步地

Useful line:

The team improves the product step by step.

## Answer Ladder

### Question 1. What is agile?

Level 1:

Agile is a flexible way of working.

Level 2:

Agile is a flexible way of working.
Teams plan, work, and improve in small steps.

Level 3:

Agile is a flexible way of working and developing products.
Instead of making one big plan and never changing it, teams work in small steps.
They learn from feedback and adjust their work over time.

### Question 2. What is scrum?

Level 1:

Scrum is one agile method.

Level 2:

Scrum is one agile method.
It helps teams organize their work more clearly.

Level 3:

Scrum is a common framework used in agile teams.
It gives the team a simple structure for planning, doing, and reviewing work.
I think it is useful because it makes teamwork more organized.

### Question 3. What is a sprint?

Level 1:

A sprint is a short work cycle.

Level 2:

A sprint is a short work cycle.
During the sprint, the team focuses on a small set of tasks.

Level 3:

A sprint is a short period of work, often one or two weeks.
During this time, the team tries to finish a clear set of tasks.
This helps the team stay focused and review progress regularly.

### Question 4. What does a scrum master do?

Level 1:

A scrum master helps the team work better.

Level 2:

A scrum master helps the team work better.
This person supports the process and removes problems.

Level 3:

A scrum master is not the boss of the team.
This person helps the team follow the scrum process, communicate better, and solve workflow problems.
In some companies, the scrum master also helps the team improve meeting quality and teamwork.

### Question 5. What do you think about agile?

Level 1:

I think it is useful.

Level 2:

I think it is useful.
It helps teams adapt to change.

Level 3:

I think agile is useful because work changes all the time.
If a team works in small steps, it can adjust more easily.
But I also think the team needs good communication, or agile may not work well.

## Guided Speaking Script

Read this script many times until you can say it without looking at every line.

Today I want to talk about agile development.
Agile is a flexible way of working.
It means teams work in small steps and improve over time.

Scrum is one common agile method.
It gives the team a simple structure.
The team plans the work, does the work, and then reviews the result.

A sprint is a short work cycle.
In a sprint, the team focuses on a small group of tasks.
This can help the team stay focused and make progress more clearly.

Some companies also have a scrum master.
A scrum master helps the team follow the process.
This person is not just checking tasks.
This person also helps communication and removes process problems.

I think agile can be useful.
It helps teams adjust to change and improve step by step.
But good teamwork and clear communication are still very important.

## Tutor Q&A Drill

### Easy Questions

What is agile?
Agile is a flexible way of working.

What is scrum?
Scrum is one common agile method.

What is a sprint?
A sprint is a short work cycle.

### Medium Questions

Why do teams use sprints?
Teams use sprints to focus on a small set of tasks and review progress regularly.

Why do some companies need a scrum master?
Because the team needs support with process, communication, and workflow problems.

What is the difference between agile and scrum?
Agile is the general idea or way of working.
Scrum is one specific method inside agile.

### Personal Questions

Do you think agile is useful?
Yes, I do.
I think it helps teams adapt and improve more quickly.

Would you like to work in a scrum team?
Yes, maybe.
I think it could be helpful if the team communicates well.

What is more important, process or communication?
I think both are important.
But without good communication, even a good process may not work well.
