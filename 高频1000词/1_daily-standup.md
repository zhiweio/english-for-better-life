# Daily Standup

## Material Goal

- 理解 daily standup 这篇短文的主要内容
- 掌握一批软件团队里高频、可复用的词和短语
- 重点练习常见工作汇报句型、发音和短篇复述

## Quick Chinese Brief

这篇短文讲的是 Scrum 团队的 daily standup。团队成员在早上开一个很短的同步会，每个人说昨天做了什么、今天要做什么、有没有 blocker。短文里出现了很多工作场景里的高频词，比如 feature、bug、review、pull request、deadline、priority、progress。

这类材料很适合练“短句汇报”和“重复高频词”。目标不是把所有技术细节讲深，而是把这些高频工作表达练熟。

## Original Text

**Theme: Scrum daily standup and progress update**

It is 9:30 in the morning. I walk into the meeting room with my laptop. My team is already there. We are having our daily standup meeting.

"Good morning, everyone. Let us get started," says Sarah, our Scrum master. "Who wants to go first?"

"I will go," says Mike. "Yesterday, I finished the login feature. I wrote some unit tests for it. Today, I will work on the API. I am going to implement the user endpoint. I have no blockers."

"Great. Thank you, Mike," says Sarah. "Who is next?"

"My turn," I say. "Yesterday, I fixed a bug in the payment module. The bug was causing the app to crash when users entered a wrong credit card number. I spent three hours debugging it. I found the issue in the validation logic. Today, I will review a pull request from Tom and then start working on a new feature. I am blocked by the database team. I need a new table for user profiles, but they have not created it yet."

"Okay, I will talk to the database team after this meeting," says Sarah. "Tom, how about you?"

"I finished the frontend for the dashboard yesterday," says Tom. "Today, I will push my changes to the remote repo and open a pull request. I need someone to review my code. After that, I will write some documentation for the new components. No blockers."

"Perfect," says Sarah. "The sprint ends on Friday. We have three more days. Let us make sure we meet the deadline. Does anyone have any questions?"

Everyone shakes their heads.

"Alright. Meeting adjourned. Let us get back to work."

We all stand up and go back to our desks. The daily standup only takes 15 minutes, but it helps the whole team align on priorities and progress.

## Core High-Frequency Words

中文提示：会议

MEE-ting

### 2. team

English meaning:
a group of people working together

中文提示：团队

Pronunciation hint:
`team` 长音要清楚

English meaning:

## Pronunciation Focus

### 1. daily standup

Stress hint:
DAI-ly STAND-up

中文提醒：两个词都有重音，尤其是 `STAND` 要有力。

Repeat drill:

We have a daily standup every morning.

### 2. validation logic

Stress hint:
vali-DA-tion LO-gic

中文提醒：长词分开读，先抓重音 `DA` 和 `LO`。

Repeat drill:

I found the issue in the validation logic.

### 3. documentation

Stress hint:
doc-u-men-TA-tion

中文提醒：重点在 `TA`，不要每个音节都一样重。

Repeat drill:

I will write documentation after the review.

### 4. priority

Stress hint:
pri-OR-i-ty

中文提醒：重点在 `OR`。结尾不要吞掉太多。

Repeat drill:

This task is a high priority.

### 5. progress

Stress hint:
PROG-ress

中文提醒：前重后轻，尾音清楚一点。

Repeat drill:

We talked about our progress today.

### 6. deadline

Stress hint:
DEAD-line

中文提醒：前面重点更明显。

Repeat drill:

We have to meet the deadline.

## Good Expressions and Grammar Patterns

### 1. Yesterday, I ..., and today, I ...

Pattern:
Yesterday, I ..., and today, I ...

中文说明：最常见的工作汇报句型。非常适合 daily standup。

Model line:

Yesterday, I fixed a bug, and today, I will review a pull request.

Drill:

Yesterday, I __________, and today, I will __________.

### 2. I am going to ...

Pattern:
I am going to ...

中文说明：表示接下来的计划，比 `will` 稍微更像已经准备去做。

Model line:

I am going to implement the user endpoint.

Drill:

I am going to __________ this afternoon.

### 3. I am blocked by ...

Pattern:
I am blocked by ...

中文说明：表达“我被某事卡住了”，是工作场景高频表达。

Model line:

I am blocked by the database team.

Drill:

I am blocked by __________.

### 4. I need someone to ...

Pattern:
I need someone to ...

中文说明：表达你需要他人支持。

Model line:

I need someone to review my code.

Drill:

I need someone to __________.

### 5. It helps the team ...

Pattern:
It helps the team ...

中文说明：用来总结一个流程、会议或工具的价值。

Model line:

It helps the team align on priorities and progress.

Drill:

It helps the team __________.

## Retelling and Output Drill

### 3-Line Retelling

The team is having a daily standup in the morning.
Each person talks about yesterday, today, and blockers.
The meeting is short, but it helps the team align.

### 5-Line Retelling

This article is about a Scrum daily standup.
Team members share what they finished yesterday and what they will do today.
One person has no blockers, but another person is blocked by the database team.
The sprint ends on Friday, so the team needs to meet the deadline.
The standup helps everyone align on priorities and progress.
