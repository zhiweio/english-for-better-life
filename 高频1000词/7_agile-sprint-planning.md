# Agile Sprint Planning

## Material Goal

- 理解 sprint planning 这篇短文的主要内容
- 掌握敏捷计划会议里的高频词、短语和讨论表达
- 重点练习估点、优先级、velocity、technical debt 等常见英语

## Quick Chinese Brief

这篇短文讲的是团队做两周 sprint 的计划。团队先看 product backlog，再根据 priority、story points 和 team velocity 决定把哪些 user story 放进这次 sprint。文中还提到 critical bug、reporting dashboard、password reset、technical debt 和 refactor。

这类材料很适合练“排优先级”和“讨论工作安排”。重点不是掌握完整敏捷理论，而是把会议里最常出现的词和表达练熟。

## Original Text

**Theme: Planning the next sprint**

It is Monday morning. We are having our sprint planning meeting for the next two weeks.

"Let us look at the product backlog," says Sarah. "We have 15 user stories in the backlog. We need to decide which ones to put in this sprint."

She shares her screen. I see a list of user stories. Each story has a title, a description, and a priority.

"This story has the highest priority," says the product owner. "A customer reported a critical bug in the checkout flow. Users cannot complete their purchases. This is blocking revenue."

"We need to fix that immediately," says Sarah. "How many story points do you think it is?"

"I estimate 3 points," says Mike. "The bug is in one function. It should be easy to fix."

"I agree. 3 points," I say.

"Okay, the bug goes into the sprint," says Sarah.

"What about the new reporting dashboard?" asks Tom. "The stakeholders are asking for it."

The product owner thinks for a moment. "That is a medium priority. It is not urgent, but it would be nice to have it by the end of the month."

"Let us estimate it," says Sarah.

The team discusses for a few minutes. The dashboard has many components. It needs a new API endpoint, a frontend page, and integration with the database.

"I say 13 points," says Mike.

"Agreed. It is a big feature," I say.

"13 points is high," says Sarah. "If we take the bug (3 points) and the dashboard (13 points), that is 16 points total. Our team's velocity is 20 points per sprint. We have room for 4 more points."

"Let us add the password reset feature," says Tom. "That is 5 points. It fits."

"Okay," says Sarah. "So the sprint will include: (1) the checkout bug fix, (2) the reporting dashboard, and (3) password reset. Everyone agrees?"

We all nod.

"What about technical debt?" asks Mike. "We need to refactor the old authentication module. It is a mess."

"We can add it to the next sprint," says Sarah. "For now, let us focus on delivering these three items."

She updates the sprint backlog. She assigns each story to a team member.

"Mike, you take the bug. Tom, you take the dashboard. And you take the password reset feature," she says to me.

"Got it," I say.

"The sprint starts today and ends in two weeks. Let us have a daily standup every morning at 9:30. Let us ship this!"

## Core High-Frequency Words

### 1. sprint planning

English meaning:
the meeting where the team plans the next sprint

中文提示：冲刺规划会

Pronunciation hint:
SPRINT PLAN-ning

Useful line:

We have sprint planning on Monday morning.

### 2. product backlog

English meaning:
the list of work items for the product

中文提示：产品待办列表

Pronunciation hint:
PROD-uct BACK-log

Useful line:

Let us review the product backlog first.

### 3. user story

English meaning:
a small piece of work that describes user value

中文提示：用户故事

Pronunciation hint:
U-ser STO-ry

Useful line:

This user story has a high priority.

### 4. priority

English meaning:
how important something is

中文提示：优先级

Pronunciation hint:
pri-OR-i-ty

Useful line:

We need to decide the priority first.

### 5. story points

English meaning:
a unit used to estimate work size

中文提示：故事点，估点

Pronunciation hint:
STO-ry POINTS

Useful line:

How many story points is this task?

### 6. velocity

English meaning:
how much work a team usually finishes in one sprint

中文提示：团队速度，团队每个 sprint 的产能

Pronunciation hint:
ve-LOC-i-ty

Useful line:

Our velocity is about 20 points per sprint.

### 7. stakeholder

English meaning:
a person who cares about the project result

中文提示：相关方，利益相关者

Pronunciation hint:
STAKE-hold-er

Useful line:

The stakeholders are asking for this feature.

### 8. technical debt

English meaning:
old code problems that should be fixed later

中文提示：技术债

Pronunciation hint:
TECH-ni-cal debt

Useful line:

We still have some technical debt in this module.

### 9. refactor

English meaning:
to improve code structure without changing what it does

中文提示：重构

Pronunciation hint:
re-FAC-tor

Useful line:

We need to refactor the authentication module.

### 10. assign

English meaning:
to give work to someone

中文提示：分配任务

Pronunciation hint:
a-SSIGN

Useful line:

Sarah assigned the story to me.

## Pronunciation Focus

### 1. sprint planning

Stress hint:
SPRINT PLAN-ning

中文提醒：两个词都短，但都要有重音。

Repeat drill:

We are having sprint planning this morning.

### 2. backlog

Stress hint:
BACK-log

中文提醒：前重后轻，常见高频词，要反复练。

Repeat drill:

Let us look at the backlog.

### 3. velocity

Stress hint:
ve-LOC-i-ty

中文提醒：重点在 `LOC`，长词先慢读。

Repeat drill:

Our velocity is 20 points per sprint.

### 4. technical debt

Stress hint:
TECH-ni-cal debt

中文提醒：前面长词抓 `TECH`，后面的 `debt` 不要漏尾音。

Repeat drill:

We still have technical debt.

### 5. authentication

Stress hint:
au-then-ti-CA-tion

中文提醒：重点在 `CA`，词比较长，先拆开读。

Repeat drill:

The authentication module needs refactoring.

### 6. stakeholders

Stress hint:
STAKE-hold-ers

中文提醒：开头清楚，结尾复数 `s` 要带出来。

Repeat drill:

The stakeholders are asking for the dashboard.

## Good Expressions and Grammar Patterns

### 1. We need to decide which ...

Pattern:
We need to decide which ...

中文说明：很适合 planning 会议里表达筛选和决策。

Model line:

We need to decide which stories to put in this sprint.

Drill:

We need to decide which __________.

### 2. How many ... do you think it is?

Pattern:
How many ... do you think it is?

中文说明：估点和评估工作量时非常高频。

Model line:

How many story points do you think it is?

Drill:

How many __________ do you think it is?

### 3. It is not urgent, but ...

Pattern:
It is not urgent, but ...

中文说明：表达优先级不高但仍然有价值。

Model line:

It is not urgent, but it would be nice to have it this month.

Drill:

It is not urgent, but __________.

### 4. We have room for ...

Pattern:
We have room for ...

中文说明：表达当前 sprint 还有容量。

Model line:

We have room for 4 more points.

Drill:

We have room for __________.

### 5. Let us focus on ...

Pattern:
Let us focus on ...

中文说明：用来收敛讨论，强调当前重点。

Model line:

Let us focus on delivering these three items.

Drill:

Let us focus on __________.

## Retelling and Output Drill

### 3-Line Retelling

The team is planning the next sprint.
They choose stories based on priority, story points, and velocity.
In the end, they agree on three items for this sprint.

### 5-Line Retelling

This article is about a sprint planning meeting.
The team reviews the backlog and discusses which stories to include.
They decide to take a critical checkout bug, a reporting dashboard, and a password reset feature.
They do not include technical debt because the sprint is already full.
Finally, Sarah updates the sprint backlog and assigns the work.

### Output Prompts

- How does a team decide what goes into a sprint?
- Why are story points and velocity useful?
- Why is technical debt often delayed?
